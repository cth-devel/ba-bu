## Information Required for Booking

When booking an appointment, please have ready:

### Essential Information

1. **Your Name**
   - Full name for the appointment

2. **Contact Number**
   - Phone number for confirmation
   - WhatsApp number preferred

3. **Preferred Service**
   - Specific service name
   - Service category
   - Multiple services if applicable

4. **Preferred Date**
   - Your preferred appointment date
   - Alternative dates if first choice unavailable

5. **Preferred Time Slot**
   - Your preferred time
   - Alternative times if first choice unavailable

### Additional Information (Optional)

- Gender category (Ladies/Gents/Unisex)
- Hair length (for hair services: S/M/L)
- Special requirements
- Previous service history
- Allergies or sensitivities

## Booking Policies

### Advance Booking

**Recommended Booking Time**:
- **Regular Services**: 1-2 days in advance
- **Bridal Services**: At least 1 month in advance
- **Premium Services**: 3-7 days in advance
- **Same-Day Booking**: Available subject to availability

### Bridal Services Booking

**Special Requirements**:
- **Advance Booking**: Minimum 1 month before wedding date
- **Consultation**: Included with all bridal packages
- **Trial Sessions**: Available (pricing separate)
- **Confirmation**: Date and time confirmation required at least 1 week before event

**Bridal Timeline Recommended**:
- **1 Month Before**: Initial consultation and trial
- **2 Weeks Before**: Facials and treatments
- **1 Week Before**: Final touch-ups
- **Wedding Day**: Complete bridal services

### Cancellation Policy

**Cancellation Guidelines**:
- Cancellations accepted up to 24 hours before appointment
- Last-minute cancellations may incur charges
- No-shows may affect future bookings

**Rescheduling**:
- Rescheduling available with 24-hour notice
- Same-day rescheduling subject to availability

## Booking Tips

### Best Practices

1. **Book in Advance**: Especially for weekends and special occasions
2. **Be Flexible**: Have alternative dates/times ready
3. **Be Specific**: Mention exact service and preferences
4. **Confirm Details**: Double-check appointment details when booking
5. **Arrive on Time**: Arrive 5-10 minutes before appointment time

### What to Avoid

- Last-minute cancellations without notice
- Booking multiple services without adequate time
- Arriving late without prior notice
- Changing services on the day of appointment
