# Services Overview

## Service Categories

BA-BU Family Salon offers comprehensive beauty and grooming services organized into the following main categories:

---

## 1. Hair Care Services

Complete hair services for all ages and genders.

**Main Services**:
- Haircuts and styling (Ladies, Men's, Kids)
- Hair coloring (Global, Highlights, Root Touchup)
- Hair treatments (Smoothing, Keratin, Botox, Hair Spa)
- Hair spa and nourishing treatments

**Price Range**: Starting from ₹150 (Kids Cut) to ₹10,000+ (Premium treatments)

**For Details**: See [Hair Care Services](./04-hair-care-services.md)

---

## 2. Skin & Body Care Services

Comprehensive skincare and body treatments.

**Main Services**:
- **Facial Treatments** - 27+ facial varieties (Normal, VLCC, Gold, Pearl, Anti-aging, etc.)
- **Cleanup & Detan** - Basic to advanced cleanup and tan removal
- **Threading Services** - Eyebrow, upper lip, full face threading
- **Waxing Services** - Arms, legs, full body waxing
- **Bleaching Services** - Face, neck, body bleaching
- **Massage Services** - Head massage, hand/foot massage, body massage
- **Pedicure & Manicure** - Normal, spa, crystal, themed treatments

**Price Range**: Starting from ₹30 (Threading) to ₹6,000+ (Premium treatments)

**For Details**: See [Skin & Body Care](./05-skin-body-care.md)

---

## 3. Bridal Services

Complete wedding packages for brides and grooms.

**Bridal Services**:
- **Bridal Packages** - Complete beauty packages
- **Bridal Makeup** - Full HD Glossy Event Makeup, Reception Makeup
- **Bridal Treatments** - Hair styling, facials, pedicure, manicure, hair spa

**Groom Services**:
- **Groom Packages** - Complete grooming packages
- **Groom Makeup** - Event makeup, reception makeup
- **Groom Treatments** - Hair styling, beard setting, facials, grooming

**Price Range**: Individual pricing for each service component

**For Details**: See [Bridal Services](./06-bridal-services.md)

---

## 4. Men's Grooming Services

Specialized services for men.

**Main Services**:
- **Men's Haircuts** - Normal cut, change of style
- **Beard Services** - Beard setting, shaving
- **Men's Hair Treatments** - Smoothing, keratin, botox
- **Men's Hair Coloring** - Henna, global coloring, highlighting
- **Men's Facial** - Cleanup, detan treatments

**Price Range**: Starting from ₹100 (Beard setting) to ₹4,000+ (Premium treatments)

**For Details**: See [Men's Grooming](./07-mens-grooming.md)
