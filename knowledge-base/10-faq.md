### What is the difference between threading and waxing?

**Threading**:
- Uses thread for precise hair removal
- Better for facial areas
- Precise shaping (eyebrows, upper lip)
- Prices: ₹30-₹300

**Waxing**:
- Uses warm/cold wax for hair removal
- Better for larger body areas
- Longer-lasting results
- Prices: ₹50-₹5,000+

Your stylist can recommend the best option based on your needs.
